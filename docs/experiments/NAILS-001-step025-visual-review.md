## Visual findings on the four held-out identities

All five styles were generated for identities `0000045`, `0000088`, `0001030` and `0001593`. The rows below refer to the final nail-only composite, while also considering the raw model crop and Base result in each contact sheet.

| Requested style | Held-out observation | Five-style gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `classic_red` | Four of four show recognizable glossy red, but the adapter is consistently darker/burgundy than the brighter target. Base already produces a similar glossy red on these hands. | PARTIAL; no clear adapter gain |
| `glossy_black` | Four of four show recognizable glossy black and plausible highlights. Base is already very similar. Some generated nails look overly dark/plastic compared with the target. | PARTIAL; little demonstrated gain |
| `nude_pink` | Four of four are brighter pink than the muted nude targets. `0000045` and `0001593` are vivid magenta; `0001030` is bubblegum pink. | FAIL |
| `french_tip` | No held-out hand has a consistent natural nail bed plus clean white tip. `0000045` produces cyan/yellow nails; `0000088` and `0001030` cover most nails in white; `0001593` includes blue/green. Base also drifts badly on this prompt. | FAIL |
| `pink_ombre` | Three hands are mostly flat pink rather than a base-to-tip gradient. `0001593` has some gradient-like variation on two nails but includes yellowish coloration and inconsistent orientation. | FAIL |

The adapter's raw crop often alters skin/background and invents rings or bracelets on hands whose originals have none. Base does this too; the adapter has not learned dependable preservation. The nail-only compositor removes those unrelated changes: an independent RGB pixel check found **zero changed pixels outside the original hard nail masks for all 20 final composites**, preserving finger count, pose, surrounding skin, background and original non-nail accessories by construction. This does not cure wrong colors/designs within the nail mask. Small nail size at 512 resolution limits fine cuticle/boundary judgment; some bright/white outputs have hard-looking cuticle and tip transitions. There is no evidence of severe out-of-mask polish leakage in the final images because it is clipped by the compositor.

For an objective but limited comparison, per-nail RGB mean absolute error to the deterministic target is lower for the adapter than Base in only **6 of 20** held-out pairs. The adapter is worse on the four-style average for Classic Red, Nude Pink, French Tip and Pink Ombre; the Glossy Black average improves only marginally (49.27 to 48.93 on a 0–255 channel scale). These pixel distances are not perceptual quality scores, but they agree with the visible lack of added style control. Raw adapter pixels outside the nail mask differ exactly from the source in 99.81% of positions on average; this includes normal diffusion reconstruction differences, so the visible invented jewelry and hand changes are the stronger preservation evidence.

The model does produce specular highlights on some red and black nails, so it is not universally copying flat renderer polygons. Pink Ombre is mostly flat and French Tip lacks coherent tip structure. The five-style adapter does not yet demonstrate a useful learned edit beyond the Base's existing simple polish capability. Simple red/black appearances transfer to four unseen identities, but the requested five-style behavior does **not** generalize across the held-out set.

## Decision and next diagnostic

Stop at step 25. Do not call this checkpoint production-ready or connect it to `/nails`; keep application inference disconnected. Additional steps are not justified by the held-out evidence alone. The checkpoint saw less than one full 80-pair training epoch according to its metadata, so inadequate exposure is possible, but the Base comparison gives no convincing evidence that the adapter is developing control for the three failed styles.

The next bounded diagnostic is **inference only**: on one or two training identities, compare Base and step-25 adapter against the same five approved targets at the same seed, guidance and inference steps. This distinguishes failure to fit the training examples from a held-out generalization problem. Inspect actual target/control pairing and whether the toolkit consumed edit controls as intended. Only then should the Project Supervisor consider a short checkpointed continuation, a revised training schedule, or a dataset change. Retain this checkpoint as `NAILS-001-pilot-step025` experimental evidence.
